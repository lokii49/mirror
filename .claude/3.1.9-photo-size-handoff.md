only blank lines. Anything under the thumbnail (words, or a line of spaces) put the new photo below
it, out of the row, so it was drawn full width. Paste, drop and Suggestions inserted under the

  lines of spaces.

  Backspace that would merge words and a photo moves the caret instead (`keepsPhotoOnItsLine`;

  stored `[[mirror-photo-0]]hi` on one line.

### The rules as built

- **Stored values** on the photo's one inline character: none, `small`, `medium`, `full`,
  `thumbnail`. `PhotoSize(stored:)` reads `thumbnail` as full (older readers).
- **Row** (`PhotoRuns.tileMatches` editor, `tileLines` reader): 2+ consecutive lines that are only a
  photo token (spaces allowed, a blank line or words end it). A landscape first photo stays full
  width (a map); the rest are square tiles.
- **Sized** (`small`/`medium`/`full`): drawn alone at that width, taken out of the row; neighbours
  stay tiles.
- **`thumbnail`**: a tile wherever it is alone on its line; in a row it's the same as no size.
- **Menu (iPhone)**: Thumbnail / Small / Medium / Full Width. Tick = drawn state. Thumbnail stores
  nothing for a row tile, `thumbnail` alone or on a row's leading map. Full Width stores `full` next
  to another photo line, nothing alone.
- **Mac picker**: same values; `inRow` = 2+ photos (all trailing); `leadsRow` = first photo and
  landscape.

**Four places hold the rule**: editor render (`NoteEditorTextView` around `PhotoRuns.tileMatches`),
iPhone menu (`photoSizeMenu`), reader (`EntryDetailView.blocks`), Mac picker (`MacPhotoTile`). First
task next session: move the decision into one function in `PhotoSizes` (input: stored value, line
alone or not, neighbours, leading landscape → output: drawn state, tick, value each option stores),
unit-test it against the matrix, and make all four call it.

## Test matrix for next session

Every case asserts three things, in the editor, in the reader after Save, and on reopening to edit:
**stored value**, **what is drawn** (tile or width share), **which menu item is ticked**. Add a
scratch-only readout of each photo's state (stored / drawn / tick) so tests assert instead of
comparing screenshots.

**Run first (where the simulator differs from the phone)**
- **Caret when a photo is added:** before the photo, on its line after it, on the next line, after
  a blank line, editor not focused.
- **Real camera photos:** EXIF-rotated portrait and landscape, HEIC.

**Photo kinds**
- portrait, landscape, square, tiny (smaller than a tile), linked map, unreadable photo

**Arrangements**
- 1, 2, 3, 4, 5+ photos
- landscape first or not
- runs split by a blank line, by words, by spaces only
- a photo sharing its line with words
- a photo first or last in the entry
- a photo inside a checklist item

**Size changes**
- each of the 4 options from each of the 4 states
- on a lone photo, and on the first, middle and last photo of a row
- two photos in one row sized differently
- all photos sized, then all back to Thumbnail

**Structure changes after sizing**
- add a photo by paperclip, paste, drop, Suggestion
- delete a neighbour (a row of 2 becomes 1), or the sized photo itself
- slide a tile out of a row, a lone photo into a row, reorder within a row
- type after or between photos; Return and Backspace between them
- undo and redo after each of the above

**Persistence**
- draft survives relaunch; Discard and undo; Save → edit → Save; duplicate; trash and restore

**Surfaces**
- iPad width, large text size (accessibility sizes)
- Mac reader; Mac picker on a lone photo, a row, a leading landscape; a Mac body edit keeps sizes

**Old versions**
- Thumbnail on a row tile stores nothing
- a 3.1.8-shaped decode ignores `photoSize` (full width, no crash)
