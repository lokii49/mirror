Branch `3.1.6`, cut 2026-10-20 from 3.1.5. Gating goes on for 15 November; submit around 5–8 November so it's approved in time. Checklist carried over from `november-paid-activation.md` (section B).

No model or field changes are planned, so no CloudKit schema deploy. If one is added, it needs a seed + "Deploy Schema Changes" before release (`CloudKitSchemaSeed`).

## Owner decisions (2026-10-20)
- Both plans, Core and Deep, launch on 15 November. Deep-only was considered and dropped: the notice already says "Core or Deep", and one free trial per Apple ID across the group would spend Core's trial on Deep.
- Free users keep reading everything already made for them: past and current daily reflections, weekly digests and monthly reports, including the free period's. Gating stops new ones being generated.
- Widgets show the last saved reflection, digest or report to a tier that can't generate it; the locked view only shows when there's nothing saved.

## The rule
For a tier that can't generate a kind of insight, its card shows the saved row or the lock, nothing else: no "N more entries", no "available Sunday", no "waiting for month end", no Try Again, since each of those promises a generation that won't come. The branch sits first in each resolver; the Core/Deep path below it is unchanged.

## Where the gates were (map, 2026-10-20, before this work)
Gates: `isSubscribed` (Core+) and `isDeep` (SubscriptionService.swift). Sentinel changes wording only; Mac is classic only.
- Daily card: InsightViewModel `resolvedNudgeState`. Free saw only its single first reflection, then `.subscriptionRequired`. Paywall sheet 1.2 s after the first one loads (InsightView `.onChange(of: nudgeState)`, `hasSeenMoreThanOneNudge`).
- Past reflections: `pastNudges` returned `[]` for Free (iOS and Mac).
- Digest: `loadWeeklyDigest` returned `.subscriptionRequired` before the cache lookup; `pastDigests` returned `[]` (iOS and Mac).
- Monthly report: past months were never gated (MonthlyReportView reads the saved row). The current month's `isDeep` guard came after the date and count gates, so Free/Core saw "waiting for month end" or "too few entries" before the lock.
- Widgets: daily `tier == core || deep`, digest `isSubscribed`, monthly `isDeep`, else a locked view linking `mirror://upgrade`. The app writes the texts to the app group whatever the tier.
- Generation gates (unchanged): daily mirrorApp `runDailyNudgeIfNeeded` (one free reflection), digest BG `isSubscribed`, report `monthlyReportIsDue(isDeep:)`, mood alert Deep, mood backfill Core+, PreGrammarInsightRegrade Core/Deep.
- Mac sidebar lock icons are cosmetic; pages open and follow the same gates.
