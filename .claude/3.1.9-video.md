# 3.1.9: playable video in entries

Decided 2026-11-06 (owner, option B): 3.1.8 ships on 15 November with a Journaling Suggestion's
video coming in as its first frame (a photo). Playable video is the first 3.1.9 feature.

## Design (agreed on the "Suggestions in Write" mockup thread)

- **Attachment:** a video sits in a row of tiles with the entry's other attachments, not inline in
  the text (an inline token would show as raw text in older versions). The tile shows the first
  frame, a small play badge in the centre and the duration bottom-right ("0:42"). It counts toward
  a suggestion's 4-picture limit; at most 1 video per suggestion.
- **Preview:** tapping the tile expands it inline, at the editor's width and the video's aspect
  ratio, capped at about 40% of the screen height, with text still visible above and below. Sound,
  a scrubber and a close button. Tapping outside or scrolling collapses it. Never autoplays.
- **Order:** a route or place map still leads; otherwise the video leads the tiles.
- **Size:** compressed to 720p; clips over 60 s come in as their first 60 s, and the tile says so.
- **Accessibility:** VoiceOver reads "Video, 42 seconds".
- **Entry points:** Journaling Suggestions videos, and (to decide) picking a video from Photos.

## What it takes

1. Model: `encryptedVideoDataStorage: Data?` with `@Attribute(.externalStorage)` and
   `videoDurationsStorage: Data?`. Nothing else in the first commit.
2. **CloudKit schema deploy by the owner in the Console before any TestFlight build carries it.**
3. Older versions keep unknown fields, but one that edits and saves the entry may drop the video on
   the server; say so in the release notes or the What's New page.
4. Measure sealing a 10–15 MB blob in place (`sealed(_:keeping:)`) and the fault cost; the
   2026-11-04 perf audit is the reference.
5. Everything that enumerates attachments: trash, archive and restore, `JournalRestoreCopy`, erase,
   duplicates, `EntryDecryptCache.signature`, Markdown/PDF export, the Entries row, Still Open,
   widgets (never decode video), the Mac reader, and Sentinel branches.
6. Player on iPhone and Mac, tile row, strings in 10 languages, VoiceOver, What's New, release notes.
7. Tests on the 14 Pro for the library path; the suggestion path needs the owner's TestFlight run.
