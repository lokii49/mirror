# iPhone Duo audit (3.1.2)

Started 2026-10-11. Plan: `3.1.2-iphone-duo.md`.

**Method:**
- An isolated copy (`.searchdev` bundle, CloudKit off, synthetic `--perfSeed` journal plus seeded insights) on the iPhone Duo simulator (Xcode 27.1, iOS 27.1).
- Driven by a throwaway XCUITest (`DuoAuditUITests`, in the copy only) and captured per screen.
- Fold state can only be changed by hand in DeviceHub (no `simctl` or accessibility control), so the owner switches poses between runs.
- The test captures the screen that is lit. Inner-screen captures come out in the panel's native orientation and are rotated for review.

## Findings
### Must-fix
- **F1. The mood/tag chip row ignores the side safe area.** On the closed screen the "Peaceful" chip rests under the clock in the status strip. On the opened screen, "Energized" passes under the Wi-Fi icon. It's an interactive row, and Apple says interactive elements must not sit behind side controls. Likely the same for the week calendar strip (F5). Fix: keep the row's background full-bleed but inset its content with the safe area (`contentMargins` / safe-area padding).
- **F2. Sentinel sidebar.**
  - `AppSidebarItem.title` is fixed to Entries / Write / Insights / Settings, so the opened Duo in Sentinel shows Classic names, not Log / Transmission / Briefing.
  - The sidebar keeps the system grey background, not Sentinel ink.
  - The Settings page is titled "Config" while its sidebar row says "Settings".
  - iPad had this too, but on the Duo every user who opens the phone sees it.

### To verify (needs another run)
- **F3. Opening or closing the phone rebuilds the whole screen** (`ContentView` swaps `TabView` ↔ `NavigationSplitView` on the size class).
  - The first fold-while-typing run lost the typed text. That run was invalid: synthetic-journal mode turns off draft saving unless `--scratchDraftStorage` is passed.
  - In the real app the draft autosaves 0.9 s after typing and is restored when Write is rebuilt (`restoreDraftFromStorage`), so text should survive.
  - Still lost by design of the swap (from code): keyboard focus, caret position, undo history, open sheets, scroll position, a pushed reader. Rerun with drafts on to confirm.
- **F4. Formatting panel on the opened Duo.** `WriteView.usesPopoverPanel` (`userInterfaceIdiom == .pad`) and `NoteEditorTextView.swift:3406` pick the iPhone path (the panel replaces the keyboard) on a 951 × 669 pt screen. It works (captured in Sentinel), but on that screen a popover off the Aa button is the iPad behaviour Apple's guidance points to. Fix: decide by available width, not idiom.

### Should-fix / design
- **F5. The week calendar strip is clipped at both edges** on the wide screen (seen in the owner's screenshot and in the captures). It reads as cut off rather than scrollable.
- **F6. Wide screen space is mostly empty.**
  - Entries rows stretch about 850 pt for 2–3 lines.
  - The reader card uses about 60% of the width.
  - Insights is one stretched column.
  
  These are the two-column opportunities in the plan (Phase 3).

### Fine as is
- Closed screen: the system puts the status bar, tab bar, toolbar buttons and back button in the right strip, and content stays clear. Write, the format panel (it spans the strip, like a keyboard), Insights, the Today card, the digest and the Mood Timeline all fit, in both themes. Sentinel's corner brackets stay clear of the strip.
- Opened screen: sidebar switching works, including from an open entry back to Write or Insights. The keyboard and Write fit. The reader's back button is in the strip. Settings is fine.
- App Lock reads the real biometry type (`AppLock.swift:61`), so the Duo should show Touch ID (not yet seen).
- No `UIScreen.main`, orientation APIs, fixed sizes or `UIWindow(frame:)` anywhere.
- UIKit has `UIHinge` / `UIHingeStatus` (closed, partiallyOpen, fullyOpen) if a pose-aware layout is ever wanted. Apple's guidance is to rely on size classes instead.
