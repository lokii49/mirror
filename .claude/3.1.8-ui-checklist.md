**3.1.8 work list (small, all correctness or accessibility):**
1. Mood charts (week card and Mood Timeline): break the line on days with no mood instead of drawing across them.
2. Week mood chart: VoiceOver data description, reusing Mood Timeline's `MoodChartDescriptor` / `MoodChartSummary`.
3. Calendar month arrows: "Previous month" / "Next month" with the shown month as the value, 44 pt targets.
4. Calendar: show how many entries a day has when there is more than one (owner picks the form, see Decisions).
5. Entry rows: a subtle pressed state.
6. Entry row titles: use the writer's own Title/Heading style first; the "short first line" guess only when the line
   doesn't end like a sentence (avoids "arbitrary auto-bold").
7. Verify on device: Insights' last card clears the tab bar; Write keeps the cursor line above the keyboard in a long entry.
8. Verify: Dynamic Type at AX sizes on Entries/Write/Insights after Paper & Ink (strip numerals are a fixed-size exception).
9. Verify: a day with several moods; 0, 1, 2 and 7 days of chart data; a week across a month boundary.

**Decisions for the owner:**

## 1. Entries screen

### P1 — Calendar and date navigation
- ✅ Preserve the minimal calendar rather than reverting to fully filled mood-colored date tiles. (8c47beb)
- ➡️ Increase the legibility of small mood indicators (thickness, spacing, and contrast). Bars are 18×4 pt with an ink ring on hard days; revisit after testers' feedback.
- ✅ Give the selected day a clearly visible but restrained background or outline. Plum outline; today is filled plum.
- ✅ Distinguish today, selected date, days with entries, and future dates using more than color alone. Today filled, selected outlined, entries = bar, future = faded numerals; difficult moods have a ring.
- 🔧 Show the count of entries for days with multiple entries. VoiceOver has it; sighted users lost the numeral with Paper & Ink.
- ✅ Define how a day with multiple moods is represented. Rule in `CalendarHeatmap.representativeMood`: the most frequent mood, ties to the latest. ➡️ Say so in the Manual.
- 🔧 Make previous/next month controls easy to tap and announce the displayed month to VoiceOver. Today "Back"/"Next", 13 pt glyphs.
- 🔧 Check calendar clipping and horizontal scrolling on smaller iPhones and with larger text. (Device check, item 8.)
- ✅ Verify weekday alignment, locale-aware first weekday, month boundaries, leap years, and time-zone changes. Calendar uses `Calendar.current`; journal-day rule (4 AM) documented in CLAUDE.md. ➡️ Add a leap-year / DST unit test.

### P1 — Entry list and hierarchy
- ✅ Retain the thin vertical mood indicator on each entry. (8c47beb, full height)
- ✅ Align date, preview, metadata, and mood consistently across entries. (35881bb)
- ✅ Reduce the date column only if the preview still has sufficient room on compact devices. 40 pt column; AX sizes stack the date above.
- ✅ Limit preview text to approximately 2–3 lines, with consistent truncation. Title + 2 lines, or 3 lines.
- 🔧 Preserve meaningful user formatting when previewing rich text; avoid arbitrary auto-bold styling. Work list item 6.
- ✅ Use clear grouping labels such as Today, Yesterday, This Week, and month headings; avoid redundant headings. (35881bb, 3a75636)
- 🔧 Make the entire entry row a comfortable tap target and give it a subtle pressed state. Whole row taps today; no pressed state.
- ➡️ Ensure long mood names, long words, and localization do not overlap the layout. Meta line wraps to 2 lines; needs a de/ru pass.
- 👤 Decide whether word count belongs in the list. (Decision above.)
- ➡️ Make empty, filtered-empty, loading, and search-no-results states useful and calm. They exist (filtered-empty offers "Show N without filters"); restyle copy for Paper & Ink.

### P2 — Filter and toolbar polish
- ➡️ Reduce the visual competition among filter chips, toolbar capsules, calendar selector, and bottom navigation. Chips outlined (8c47beb); the toolbar capsule is iOS 26 system glass.
- ✅ Provide a clear active state and an easy way to clear mood filters. Selected chip tinted; active-filters row with Clear.
- ✅ Ensure bookmark, sort, search, and filter icons have labels and predictable actions. Labelled (UI tests query them by name).
- ➡️ Review contrast for inactive controls and future dates. Future numerals are `.quaternary`, below 4.5:1 by design (not actionable); recheck with Increase Contrast.
- ✗ Standardize copy: "35-day streak", "77 entries". Those numbers are the mockup's; the app already says "7 Day streak", "18 entries" from one plural catalog.

## 2. Write screen

### P1 — Distraction-free writing
- ✅ Keep the prominent serif day heading in the initial empty state. (8c47beb, `WriteDateTitle`)
- ✅ When editing begins, reduce or collapse secondary prompts and nonessential controls. Starter chip and Suggestions chip leave once there's text; focus mode hides the rest.
- 🔧 Keep the text cursor and current paragraph visible above the keyboard. (Device check, item 7.)
- ✅ Avoid bottom-navigation overlap with the keyboard or writing area. The tab bar sits under the keyboard; the format bar rides on it.
- ✅ Ensure the writing canvas expands naturally for long entries. (UITextView editor; perf audit 2026-11-04 at 50k characters.)
- ➡️ Support Dynamic Type and keyboard accessibility without clipped text. Done in the 2026-10-24 UI pass; recheck after Paper & Ink.
- 👤 Test scrolling, selection, paste, undo/redo, and returning from background during a long writing session. TestFlight session.

### P1 — Prompts and suggestions
- ➡️ Consolidate "Need a starting point?" and "Suggestions from your day" into a calmer hierarchy. Two quiet rows today, blank entry only; merging them is a 3.1.9 design.
- ➡️ Consider a single inline prompt with a refresh action instead of multiple prominent buttons. (With the line above.)
- ✅ Make prompt insertion explicit: never unexpectedly overwrite existing writing. Templates/suggestions fill a blank entry only; after writing, Suggestions append at the end (f325644). ➡️ Insert at the cursor.
- ✅ Keep prompts dismissible and avoid repeatedly showing them after writing begins.
- ✅ Label photo/day suggestions clearly and request permissions only when the user invokes the feature. Apple's own sheet; nothing is read until a pick.
- ✅ Ensure AI-assisted prompts do not write journal content on the user's behalf without a deliberate action. Follow-up chip is opt-in (tap "Use").

### P0/P1 — Save and metadata behavior
- ✅ Make save behavior unambiguous. "Saving…" → "Draft saved" (or Retry) plus a manual Save.
- ✅ Never show a misleading saved state before persistence succeeds. `draftSaveState = saved && attachmentsSaved ? .saved : .failed` (WriteView+Actions).
- 👤 Test interruption, force quit, offline use, failed writes, and draft recovery. Covered by unit tests for the store; a device pass is on the owner's TestFlight list.
- ✅ Make the mood selector discoverable and optional. Dashed "Mood" pill, optional.
- ✗ Use "Add to folder" when no folder is chosen, instead of "Unfiled". The app's word is collection, and "Unfiled" is a value the menu also offers; changing it touches Organize, filters and 10 languages for little gain.
- ✅ Ensure date/time editing has clear affordances. Chevron on the date; the 4 AM journal-day rule is documented.
- ✅ Keep tags easy to add/remove, including long tag names and duplicate prevention.
- ✅ Consider an optional serif body font while retaining a readable sans option. Aa › font (System, Serif, Rounded, Mono).

## 3. Insights screen

### P0/P1 — Mood chart integrity
- ✅ Define the chart's Y-axis meaning. Valence score of the mood (Low / Mid / High), `MoodLog` scores. ➡️ One line in the Manual.
- ✅ Specify how multiple entries/moods on one day are represented. Line = daily mean (`MoodChartSeries.dailyMeans`); dots = every reading.
- ✅ Clearly distinguish the chart line from individual mood-colored dots. Plum line, mood-coloured dots with a ring on hard moods.
- 🔧 Do not interpolate across missing days. Both charts draw across gaps today (work list item 1).
- ✅ Make each plotted day selectable, showing date, mood(s), and relevant entries. Hold a dot: the card names it and previews the entry.
- 🔧 Provide a text summary or accessible data list for VoiceOver. Mood Timeline has one; the week card doesn't (item 2).
- ✅ Ensure mood colors are not the sole information channel. Ring on difficult moods; names in chips and tooltips.
- 🔧 Test 0, 1, 2, and 7 days of data, multiple entries per day, and a week crossing month boundaries. (Item 9.)
- ➡️ Review chart contrast, touch targets, axis labels, and responsive layout. Dots are small hold targets.

### P1 — Reflection cards and states
- ✅ Keep the Daily Reflection card's warm-paper treatment and restrained plum accents. (8c47beb hero card)
- ✅ Clarify when a reflection is scheduled, generating, ready, unavailable, or failed. Needs-more-entries, generating, honest fallback and locked cards exist.
- ✅ Prefer precise status text over a countdown. No countdown; "Ready in the last week" style copy.
- ✅ Make "Past reflections" expansion state obvious. Show all row (3.1.8).
- ✅ Differentiate daily and weekly sections with typography and spacing, not borders. Serif section headings (8c47beb).
- ✅ Avoid implying a reflection was generated if the process hasn't completed. Fallback is never saved over a real row.
- ✅ Ensure generated insights can be traced back to the user's own entries. Quotes are verbatim; Settings › About › "Show how insights are made" (9865c79, fc6d3a6).

## 4. Shared visual system

### P1 — Typography and spacing
- ✅ Use serif typography for editorial headings, prominent dates, and optionally journal prose. (8c47beb, dbf773d)
- ✅ Use sans-serif for buttons, labels, metadata, chart annotations, and compact controls.
- ➡️ Establish consistent type tokens for screen title, section heading, card heading, body, and caption. Sizes are per-view today (`scaledFont`); a token table is a 3.1.9 refactor.
- ➡️ Standardize horizontal screen margins, card padding, row spacing, and corner radii. Mostly 16/20 pt, radii 18–22; audit in 3.1.9.
- ✅ Keep enough contrast between primary, secondary, placeholder, and disabled text. textSecondary 4.9:1 light / 5.6:1 dark on paper.
- ➡️ Test at larger accessibility text sizes and with bold text enabled. (Item 8 covers AX sizes for 3.1.8; Bold Text in 3.1.9.)

### P2 — Surfaces, borders, and color
- ✅ Preserve warm off-white backgrounds and muted plum as the primary accent.
- ➡️ Audit every outlined pill, toolbar, and card; remove borders that do not clarify interaction or hierarchy.
- ✅ Use shadows sparingly and consistently; avoid heavy floating-card effects. One soft shadow; one hero lift per screen.
- ✅ Keep mood colors semantically consistent across Entries, calendar, Write, and Insights. One table (`MirrorTheme.moodColor`), mirrored in widgets.
- ✅ Check color contrast against WCAG guidance. Measured for text tokens; mood colours validated 2026-10-21.
- ✅ Avoid introducing unrelated gradients, glossy effects, or excessive accent colors. Plum glows removed (8c47beb). ➡️ Mood Timeline's line gradient and score bar still use two colours.

### P1 — Bottom navigation
- ✅ Slightly reduce height or visual weight if it obstructs content. iOS 26 floating tab bar; system-sized.
- ✅ Preserve a clear selected state for Entries, Write, and Insights.
- 🔧 Ensure scroll content has enough bottom inset so the last item is never hidden. Entries has 96 pt; Insights to verify (item 7).
- ✅ Adapt navigation appropriately when the keyboard is open.
- ➡️ Check safe areas, home indicator, VoiceOver labels, and minimum tap targets.
- ✅ Verify tab state retention when switching between screens.

## 6. Accessibility, privacy, and quality gates

### P0 — Accessibility
- ✅ Verify minimum comfortable touch targets (44 × 44 pt). Rows and chips; 🔧 calendar arrows are the known exception (item 3).
- ➡️ Test VoiceOver order, accessible names, values, and hints for icon-only controls. Labels exist; a full VoiceOver walk is 3.1.9.
- ➡️ Support Dynamic Type without truncating essential actions. (Item 8 for 3.1.8 screens.)
- ➡️ Test Increase Contrast, Reduce Transparency, Reduce Motion, and Bold Text settings. Reduce Motion is honoured (zoom transitions, scroll); the others are untested.
- ✅ Provide non-color indicators for mood states and charts. Ring + names.
- ➡️ Confirm focus behavior when modals, menus, and the keyboard open or close.

### P0 — Privacy and reliability
- ✅ Verify private journal content never appears in unintended previews, logs, analytics, or screenshots. No analytics; journal text never logged (CLAUDE.md rule); previews only on a deliberate long press. ➡️ App-switcher snapshot is covered only with App Lock on.
- ✅ Make any photo/day suggestion access permission-scoped and explain why. Apple's picker; Settings › Journal explains.
- ✅ Confirm offline writing and local save/recovery matches the privacy promises. All on device; drafts sealed locally.
- ✅ Ensure generated insights are based on available user entries and communicate missing-data limitations. Needs-more-entries card; honest fallback.
- 👤 Test app restart, device rotation, time-zone changes, and low-storage scenarios.

### P1 — User validation
- 👤 Run a five-person usability test with both existing and new journal users.
- 👤 Ask participants to find an entry from a particular day, write and save an entry, and interpret a mood chart point.
- 👤 Observe completion time, mis-taps, hesitation, and comprehension.
- ✗ Compare Purple and Plum & Paper in a preference test. One theme now (see §5).
- ➡️ Track whether the editorial style helps users feel calm and confident, while preserving task speed. With the usability test.
