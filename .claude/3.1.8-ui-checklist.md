1. Mood charts (week card and Mood Timeline): break the line on days with no mood instead of drawing across them.
2. Week mood chart: VoiceOver data description, reusing Mood Timeline's `MoodChartDescriptor` / `MoodChartSummary`.

## 1. Entries screen

- ✅ Preserve the minimal calendar rather than reverting to fully filled mood-colored date tiles. (8c47beb)
- ✅ Increase the legibility of small mood indicators (thickness, spacing, and contrast). Bars 20×5 pt (was 18×4) with an ink ring on hard days.
- ✅ Give the selected day a clearly visible but restrained background or outline. Plum outline; today is filled plum.
- ✅ Distinguish today, selected date, days with entries, and future dates using more than color alone. Today filled, selected outlined, entries = bar, future = faded numerals; difficult moods have a ring.
- ✅ Show the count of entries for days with multiple entries. Small count above the day (owner: small count) (7932e01).
- ✅ Define how a day with multiple moods is represented. Rule in `CalendarHeatmap.representativeMood`: the most frequent mood, ties to the latest. Manual step "How your moods are drawn".
- ✅ Make previous/next month controls easy to tap and announce the displayed month to VoiceOver. 44 pt, month as the value (7932e01).
- ✅ Check calendar clipping and horizontal scrolling on smaller iPhones and with larger text. AX3, de, ru checked on the 14 Pro; the week strip scrolls.
- ✅ Verify weekday alignment, locale-aware first weekday, month boundaries, leap years, and time-zone changes. Calendar uses `Calendar.current`; journal-day rule (4 AM) documented in CLAUDE.md. DST tests found and fixed a real `journalDay` bug (7932e01); chart DST week test.

### P1 — Entry list and hierarchy

- ✅ Limit preview text to approximately 2–3 lines, with consistent truncation. Title + 2 lines, or 3 lines.
- ✅ Preserve meaningful user formatting when previewing rich text; avoid arbitrary auto-bold styling. Writer's own Title/Heading first; guessed title only for a line that doesn't end like a sentence (7932e01).

- ✅ Ensure long mood names, long words, and localization do not overlap the layout. de/ru Entries, Insights, Write checked on device; Write's date wraps at AX sizes.
- 👤 Decide whether word count belongs in the list. (Decision above.)
- ✅ Make empty, filtered-empty, loading, and search-no-results states useful and calm. Serif "No matches" + why + one action (Search without filters / Clear filters / Clear search); serif empty journal title.

## 2. Write screen

### P1 — Distraction-free writing
- ✅ Keep the prominent serif day heading in the initial empty state. (8c47beb, `WriteDateTitle`)
- ✅ When editing begins, reduce or collapse secondary prompts and nonessential controls. Starter chip and Suggestions chip leave once there's text; focus mode hides the rest.
- ✅ Keep the text cursor and current paragraph visible above the keyboard. 30-line draft with the cursor at the end (DEBUG `--seedLongDraft`): the last line sits above the format bar at default, AX3, German and Russian on the 14 Pro.
- ✅ Avoid bottom-navigation overlap with the keyboard or writing area. The tab bar sits under the keyboard; the format bar rides on it.
- ✅ Ensure the writing canvas expands naturally for long entries. (UITextView editor; perf audit 2026-11-04 at 50k characters.)
- ✅ Support Dynamic Type and keyboard accessibility without clipped text. AX3 Write checked on device: date wraps to two lines; word count and draft status stay at the default size so the collection/tag row keeps its room; the prompt scrolls under the header while the caret stays in view.
- 👤 Test scrolling, selection, paste, undo/redo, and returning from background during a long writing session. TestFlight session.

### P1 — Prompts and suggestions
- ✅ Consolidate "Need a starting point?" and "Suggestions from your day" into a calmer hierarchy. Option B (6badff0).
- ✅ Consider a single inline prompt with a refresh action instead of multiple prominent buttons. Prompt + ↻ + Use this prompt + More ways to start (6badff0).
- ✅ Make prompt insertion explicit: never unexpectedly overwrite existing writing. Templates/suggestions fill a blank entry only; after writing, Suggestions go in after the cursor's paragraph (6badff0).
- ✅ Keep prompts dismissible and avoid repeatedly showing them after writing begins.
- ✅ Label photo/day suggestions clearly and request permissions only when the user invokes the feature. Apple's own sheet; nothing is read until a pick.
- ✅ Ensure AI-assisted prompts do not write journal content on the user's behalf without a deliberate action. Follow-up chip is opt-in (tap "Use").

### P0/P1 — Save and metadata behavior
- ✅ Make save behavior unambiguous. "Saving…" → "Draft saved" (or Retry) plus a manual Save.
- ✅ Never show a misleading saved state before persistence succeeds. `draftSaveState = saved && attachmentsSaved ? .saved : .failed` (WriteView+Actions).
- 👤 Test interruption, force quit, offline use, failed writes, and draft recovery. Covered by unit tests for the store; a device pass is on the owner's TestFlight list.
- ✅ Make the mood selector discoverable and optional. Dashed "Mood" pill, optional.
- ✗ Use "Add to folder" when no folder is chosen, instead of "Unfiled". The app's word is collection, and "Unfiled" is a value the menu also offers; changing it touches Organize, filters and 10 languages for little gain.
- ✅ Ensure date/time editing has clear affordances. Chevron on the date; the 4 AM journal-day rule is documented.
- ✅ Keep tags easy to add/remove, including long tag names and duplicate prevention.
- ✅ Consider an optional serif body font while retaining a readable sans option. Aa › font (System, Serif, Rounded, Mono).

## 3. Insights screen

- ✅ Specify how multiple entries/moods on one day are represented. Line = daily mean (`MoodChartSeries.dailyMeans`); dots = every reading.
- ✅ Clearly distinguish the chart line from individual mood-colored dots. Plum line, mood-coloured dots with a ring on hard moods.
- ✅ Do not interpolate across missing days. `MoodChartSeries.runs` (7932e01).
- ✅ Make each plotted day selectable, showing date, mood(s), and relevant entries. Hold a dot: the card names it and previews the entry.
- ✅ Provide a text summary or accessible data list for VoiceOver. Week card has `MoodChartDescriptor` + summary (7932e01).
- ✅ Ensure mood colors are not the sole information channel. Ring on difficult moods; names in chips and tooltips.
- ✅ Test 0, 1, 2, and 7 days of data, multiple entries per day, and a week crossing month boundaries. `MoodChartRunsTests` (8 tests, incl. two DST weeks).
- ✅ Review chart contrast, touch targets, axis labels, and responsive layout. Hold anywhere on the plot picks the nearest dot (`MoodDotScrubOverlay`); axis words no longer cut to "Hi…" under Bold Text.

### P1 — Reflection cards and states
- ✅ Keep the Daily Reflection card's warm-paper treatment and restrained plum accents. (8c47beb hero card)
- ✅ Clarify when a reflection is scheduled, generating, ready, unavailable, or failed. Needs-more-entries, generating, honest fallback and locked cards exist.
- ✅ Prefer precise status text over a countdown. No countdown; "Ready in the last week" style copy.
- ✅ Make "Past reflections" expansion state obvious. Show all row (3.1.8).

## 4. Shared visual system

### P1 — Typography and spacing
- ✅ Use serif typography for editorial headings, prominent dates, and optionally journal prose. (8c47beb, dbf773d)
- ✅ Use sans-serif for buttons, labels, metadata, chart annotations, and compact controls.
- ✅ Establish consistent type tokens for screen title, section heading, card heading, body, and caption. Audited sizes, not a refactor: serif headings 20–21, labels 11.5 caps, body 14–15; the only sub-9 pt text is the Year grid. Pinned label was the outlier (fixed).
- ✅ Standardize horizontal screen margins, card padding, row spacing, and corner radii. Full-width cards are 22 pt everywhere (20/24/26 outliers fixed); compact cards 14–18.
- ✅ Keep enough contrast between primary, secondary, placeholder, and disabled text. textSecondary 4.9:1 light / 5.6:1 dark on paper.
- ✅ Test at larger accessibility text sizes and with bold text enabled. AX3 + Bold Text on Entries/Write/Insights on device (DEBUG `--boldText`, `--increaseContrast`).

### P2 — Surfaces, borders, and color
- ✅ Preserve warm off-white backgrounds and muted plum as the primary accent.
- ✅ Audit every outlined pill, toolbar, and card; remove borders that do not clarify interaction or hierarchy. Tag chips lost theirs; mood chips (filter state) and the dashed Mood pill (empty slot) keep theirs.
- ✅ Use shadows sparingly and consistently; avoid heavy floating-card effects. One soft shadow; one hero lift per screen.
- ✅ Keep mood colors semantically consistent across Entries, calendar, Write, and Insights. One table (`MirrorTheme.moodColor`), mirrored in widgets.
- ✅ Check color contrast against WCAG guidance. Measured for text tokens; mood colours validated 2026-10-21.
- ✅ Avoid introducing unrelated gradients, glossy effects, or excessive accent colors. Plum glows removed (8c47beb); Mood Timeline's line is plum only; its score bar is a plain track with a plum dot and the Avg. score pill is plum (both were red/orange/green).

### P1 — Bottom navigation
- ✅ Slightly reduce height or visual weight if it obstructs content. iOS 26 floating tab bar; system-sized.
- ✅ Preserve a clear selected state for Entries, Write, and Insights.
- ✅ Ensure scroll content has enough bottom inset so the last item is never hidden. Entries 96 pt; Insights' last card clears the tab bar (device, 7932e01).
- ✅ Adapt navigation appropriately when the keyboard is open.
- ✅ Check safe areas, home indicator, VoiceOver labels, and minimum tap targets. Device captures; empty-state actions and Search without filters are 44 pt; chips announce Selected.
- ✅ Verify tab state retention when switching between screens.
