mirror

Translation review

mirror is a private journaling app. Its on-device AI picks one of the writer's own sentences and quotes it back. It never makes up facts. The app then adds a short, fixed line around the quote. Those fixed lines were translated without a native speaker. Please check whether they sound natural, warm and correct in your language.

About 15 minutes. Nothing leaves this page until you copy your answers and send them back.
