Smarter search in Ask, reflection styles and a steadier editor

• Smarter search in Ask (optional): find entries by meaning, not only matching words. It's a one-time 334 MB download on Wi-Fi that runs on your device, and your journal never leaves it. Turn it on in Ask or in Settings.
• Ask finds better matches even without it: whole words count, and rare words count for more than common ones.
• Reflection styles (Core): choose how today's reflection reads. Gentle as before, Quiet with only your own words, or Curious with a question about what you wrote. Choose it in Settings.
• Writing: the keyboard opens straight away, the cursor starts at the end of your draft, and a draft keeps its date, font, tags and formatting.
• Formatting: the Aa panel is redesigned so every control fits on screen, including with large text, and checklist actions are in one menu.
• Lists and checklists: Backspace at a marker leaves the list, Return on an empty item ends it, bold and italic stay where you put them, and undo works across formatting.
• Typing in Japanese, Chinese and Korean is handled better in the editor.
• Long entries and entries with photos are quicker to edit.
• Tags: typing #work, or several tags separated by commas, now does what you'd expect.
• Export and import now include your saved views.
• Search words are highlighted inside voice-note transcripts too.
• On-device AI model download (for devices without Apple Intelligence): Pause and Resume, and Try Again after a lost connection, now carry on from where they stopped. The model now comes from our own server and is checked against a fixed fingerprint before it's used.
