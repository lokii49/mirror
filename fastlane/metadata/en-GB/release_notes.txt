Safer journals, and a fuller daily reflection

• If you turn off iCloud for MirrorNotes or sign out of iCloud, your iPhone now keeps a private copy of your journal and offers to restore any entries that disappear.
• A notice tells you when your latest changes haven't reached iCloud yet, so you know not to turn iCloud off before they're backed up.
• If some entries can't be read on a new device, MirrorNotes now tells you why and how to fix it (turn on iCloud Keychain).
• Delete Everything now also removes mood check-ins, and your other devices won't offer the deleted entries back.
• Your daily reflection now also shows a second sentence from your entry, in your own words (English).
• On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, days spent at a hospital, clinic or similar now get a reflection instead of none.
• A new Format panel, laid out like Notes: styles, bold/italic/underline/strikethrough, lists, indents and colours in one place.
