Daily mood check-in, home screen widgets, and a sharper editor.

• Daily mood check-in — log how you're feeling in one tap, no writing needed. If you miss your reminder, MirrorNotes asks next time you open the app, and a Log mood button on Insights lets you update anytime. Every check-in feeds your Mood Timeline and the Mood Map widget.
• Weekly & Monthly widgets — your latest Weekly Digest and Monthly Deep Report right on the home screen.
• Last week's digest stays visible until this week's is ready, and past digests now have their own archive — just like reflections.
• Sharper reflections — Daily Nudges and Ask answers get straight to the point, no filler openers.
• Editor: type "1. " to start a numbered list the way Notes does it, with cleaner spacing. Return continues the list, and continues out of it cleanly.
• Fixes and polish.
