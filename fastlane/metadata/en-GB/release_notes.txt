• Still Open widget: the checklist items you haven't ticked, on your Home Screen. Tick them there and they're ticked in their entries when you open MirrorNotes. Its buttons start a checklist, a numbered list or a reminder on every plan; the list needs Core or Deep.
• Photo sizes: hold a photo and choose Thumbnail, Small, Medium or Full Width. Thumbnails sit side by side, three to a row, while you write and when you read.
• Hold a photo and slide it to move it, between thumbnails too. The paperclip adds photos where your cursor is.
• Copy or cut text with photos in it, and the photos come along when you paste it in MirrorNotes.
• Fixes: a crash when MirrorNotes went into the background, Redo after Undo, and a doubled letter when typing right after a photo.
