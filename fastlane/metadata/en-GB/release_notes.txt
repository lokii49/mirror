Fixes for smarter search, writing and check-ins

• Smarter search in Ask: the search model no longer downloads again after you reopen the app. It now downloads in the background over Wi-Fi or mobile data, and you can pause and resume it.
• Model downloads show a clearer progress bar with one Pause/Resume button, and a clearer Download button.
• Writing: choosing Title, Heading or Subheading in an empty entry now applies to what you type.
• Mood check-in: every mood name fits its button, and each mood keeps its colour.
• What's New shows only the latest changes, not every past release.
• The rating and mood check-in sheets now fit their content.
