Made for iPhone Duo, and a Write screen like Notes

• iPhone Duo: open it flat and the list sits beside the entry you're reading, while Insights spreads into two columns. Half-folded, lists and writing stay off the crease. Folding or unfolding keeps your writing and undo history.
• Write has a new keyboard bar and Aa panel, laid out like Apple Notes. Checklists work like Notes too: tap a circle to tick it, swipe to indent, drag to reorder, and done items can move to the bottom.
• Small animations show what just happened: something to save, a draft kept, your daily word goal reached.
• With Apple Intelligence, Weekly Digest, Monthly Report and Ask Mirror now quote your own sentences.
• Insights has dated headers and cleaner cards. On iOS 18 and later, press and hold a day in a mood chart to see what you wrote.
• Brain View groups your words into topics.
• The Mood Map widget can show 7 or 14 days.
• On iPad and an open iPhone Duo, Settings opens from the person button in Insights.
