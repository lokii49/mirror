What went wrong with reflections, and what's different now

Several earlier updates said reflections were fixed. They weren't, and I'm sorry.

This time I found the cause. On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, mirror's built-in AI wrote like a storyteller and made up details: rain that wasn't there, people swapped around.

On those iPhones, starting now:
• Reflections, weekly digests, monthly reports and Ask quote your own sentences word for word. Anything in quotation marks is exactly what you wrote, so you can check it against your entry.
• Apart from the quote, there's one short line about how it sounds. That line can still misread your mood or get a small detail wrong.
• If Ask can't find your topic, it shows the closest things you wrote instead of guessing.

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device model still writes in its own words.

Also in this update:
• Weekly digests now arrive on Sundays in regions where the week starts on Sunday.
• The home screen widget and notifications show only the short line, never your own sentence.
• Try Again now waits for a reflection that's still being written, instead of doing nothing.
• Fixed characters sometimes going missing in Japanese, Korean and Chinese AI text.
• Reflections created before this update aren't changed.

If a reflection ever gets something wrong, please tell me: Settings → About → Send feedback.
