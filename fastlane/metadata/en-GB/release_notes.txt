Clearer reflections, and a fix for Japanese and Chinese

• On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, your daily reflection now quotes your own words, then adds one short line. In English that line is checked against what you wrote, so details you never wrote no longer appear. Other languages show your quote with a fixed line chosen by your mood.
• Reflection openings now alternate between "You wrote," and "Earlier you wrote," so they don't all start the same way.
• The first entry in onboarding now counts Japanese and Chinese words correctly.
• On a new device, the Japanese and Chinese word-count update now waits until your entries have finished syncing from iCloud.
• The Low, Mid and High labels on the mood charts are now translated.
