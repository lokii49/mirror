Lock your journal, find anything, keep it organised

• App Lock: protect MirrorNotes with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. It locks when the app opens and after 5 minutes away, and hides your journal in the app switcher. Turn it on in Settings.
• Better search: put words in "quotes" for an exact phrase, type tag:work to search a tag, or -word to leave a word out. Matching words are highlighted, and results can be sorted by best match.
• Filters: combine several moods, any or all of your tags, date ranges (including This week and This month), photos, voice notes and pinned entries.
• Collections: group entries into collections and switch between them in Entries. Each entry can be in one collection.
• Saved views: save a search with its filters and sort, and open it again with one tap.
• Safer drafts: your unsaved draft is fully encrypted on your iPhone, the editor shows whether it's been kept, and unsaved changes to an entry can be restored next time you open it.
• Export your whole journal as a ZIP of Markdown files with your photos and voice recordings. You can import an unzipped archive back; existing entries are never overwritten.
