More reliable digests, widgets and translations

• Weekly Digest and Monthly Report now arrive more reliably, and a failed retry never replaces one you already have.
• Widgets no longer show a lock and a price. The streak and map widgets update as soon as you save, and the digest and monthly widgets show last week's and last month's until the new one arrives.
• Delete Everything now also clears your text from widgets and reminders.
• Editing an entry that has only a voice note no longer risks damaging it.
• Reflections and entries follow your text size, and VoiceOver reads calendar days and buttons clearly.
• Mood Timeline draws one point per day, so busy days no longer leave stray shapes in the chart.
• More of the app is translated, including widgets, Siri phrases and notifications.
