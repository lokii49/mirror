Reflections after you write, and what they read

• Write in the evening and you now get a reflection that day. If today's reflection was about earlier days and you then write today, a second one about today can appear. A reflection about an earlier day says which day it's from.
• Reflections now wait for the exact minute you set, not just the hour.
• On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, the reflection is a quote from your entry plus one plain sentence about how you seem. Only on harder days does it add one short, fixed suggestion. The generic "take a few deep breaths" is gone.
• On those iPhones, the follow-up question that appears while you write now quotes your own words, so it can't bring in things you didn't write.
• On any reflection, you can see exactly what was read to write it.
• On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, if your latest weekly digest or monthly report was written by an earlier version, it may be rewritten once. Older ones aren't changed.
• Questions in Ask can be up to 500 characters.
• If mirror can't open your journal, it now says so, with a Try Again button, instead of crashing. Nothing is deleted.
• Japanese and Chinese entries now have proper word counts, so totals, daily goals and XP make sense.

If a reflection gets something wrong, please tell me: Settings → About → Send feedback.
