Lock your journal, capture from anywhere, find anything

• App Lock: protect MirrorNotes with Touch ID or your Mac password. It locks when the app opens and after 5 minutes away, and covers your journal while locked. Turn it on in Settings.
• Quick capture from any app: press Option-Command-J to jot something down without leaving what you're doing. Change the shortcut or turn it off in Settings.
• Your daily check-in reminder now shows even while MirrorNotes is open, and clicking it takes you straight to Log Mood.
• Better search: put words in "quotes" for an exact phrase, type tag:work to search a tag, or -word to leave a word out. Matching words are highlighted, and results can be sorted by best match.
• Filters: combine several moods, any or all of your tags, date ranges (including This week and This month), photos and pinned entries.
• Collections: group entries into collections and switch between them in Entries. Each entry can be in one collection.
• Saved views: save a search with its filters and sort, and open it again in one click.
• Safer drafts: the editor shows whether your draft has been kept, and unsaved changes to an entry, even ones closed with Esc, can be restored next time you open it.
• Export your whole journal as a ZIP of Markdown files with your photos. You can import an unzipped archive back; existing entries are never overwritten.
