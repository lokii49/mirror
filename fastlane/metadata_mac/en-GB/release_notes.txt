Core and Deep are here

• New daily reflections, Ask, new weekly digests and monthly reports, the mood timeline and widgets now need Core or Deep. Writing, reading, search and iCloud backup stay free, forever. New subscribers get a 7-day free trial.
• Everything already made for you stays readable on every plan: past reflections, digests and reports, in the app and in widgets.

Also in this update

• If plans can't load, Subscription offers Try Again instead of loading forever.
• Past reflections on the board have Read more, and past reflections and digests end with Show all.
• Tapping a day in the Entries calendar keeps that day in view instead of jumping back to today.
• Organize can add a Scratch collection for quick notes, lists and numbers. Reflections, Ask, mood charts and mood alerts don't read it, and any collection can be kept out of them the same way.
• Big journals are faster: the Entries list, search and Insights no longer re-read every entry whenever the window changes.
